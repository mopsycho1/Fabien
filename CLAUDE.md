# Fabien: Of Gods & Men

A Final Fantasy Tactics style tactics RPG in the browser. Baiyee (IT background, beginner at games) builds it with Claude; his cousin Suleiman designs it and draws the art. Live at https://mopsycho1.github.io/Fabien/ (GitHub Pages deploys on every push to main).

## How to work with Baiyee
- Plain language. No em dashes. Write ranges as "X to Y". Keep code commented and simple.
- Find the cause before fixing. Story and dialogue text stays in JSON (src/data).
- Before each batch, name one Jesse Schell lens (Art of Game Design) in 2 or 3 sentences and why.
- "The best feasible option wins": when a choice is open, pick what makes battles feel most premium and is realistic to build, and say what you picked.
- One batch at a time: build, test, tick the roadmap, push, then stop and wait.
- Keep replies short. Draft a WhatsApp update for Suleiman at the end of a batch when it helps.

## Roadmap (docs/ROADMAP.md)
- Read only the sections you need (grep for the item numbers). Every new request gets a number in the right phase; nothing is dropped quietly.
- Log every file received in "Files received" (bottom) the turn it arrives: date, time, what it is, which item.
- Tick items when built and tested, with a short "Built:" note.

## Save tokens
- Don't re-read big files whole: grep, then read the lines you need.
- Look at many pictures at once on one contact sheet (a small Python/PIL script), not one by one.
- Run only the tests for what changed; run the full suite once before pushing.
- Don't paste long logs back; filter to PASS/FAIL lines.

## Commands
- Play locally: `npm install` once, then `npm run dev` (or `npx vite build` and `npx vite preview --port 4173`).
- Tests (jsdom, in dev/tests): `cd dev/tests && npm install` once. Each run: start the picture server in another terminal, `node serve.cjs` (in dev/tests; the old python server drops pictures on Mac), then `bash bundle.sh` after any code change, then `node <test>.cjs`.
  - Most tests use the old 8x8 map (yoba). Cavern tests: `FABIEN_MAP=cavern FABIEN_BENCH= node n41.cjs` (n41 to n45, n53, n54, n56), `FABIEN_MAP=cavern FABIEN_BENCH=e1 node n39.cjs`.
  - Full suite: `bash dev/tests/run_all.sh` (starts the picture server, bundles, runs every test with its settings 4 at a time, prints only failures and a final count; about 3 to 4 minutes). A test that fails beside the others is rerun alone and listed as SHAKY. `JOBS=1` runs one at a time. Results per test land in dev/tests/last_run.txt.
  - Steady check: `bash dev/tests/steady.sh` runs the suite 3 times and says if the runs differ.
  - Bot battles (balance): `FABIEN_MAP=cavern node bot.cjs 2`. It also prints OFF TILE if a figure is left standing off the middle of its tile (13.47). FABIEN_BENCH=<ids> picks who sits out, NO_ENCHANT=1 turns Enchant off. It ends with the median turns and every skill and item each fighter used. `FABIEN_BENCH=e2,h4` gives the old 3 v 3.
- Browser checks (Playwright): `node dev/browser/<name>.cjs <out dir>` with the preview running.
  - Whole battles played by a test player: `node dev/browser/battle.cjs [phone|desktop|both] [how many]` lists page errors; `node dev/browser/jumps.cjs [phone|desktop]` is the jump logger (a figure moving over one tile in 80 ms, at 60 frames a second). Both end with ALL PASSED or FAILED. `node dev/browser/sizes.cjs <out dir> [phone|desktop]` photographs every fighter in a list of poses (size ruler check). `node dev/browser/gifs.cjs <out dir> [phone|desktop] [names]` films moves as GIFs at game speed (the list of moves is at the top of the file). Add `effects` in place of names to film all 33 skills and statuses; `gif_pair.py <before> <after> <out>` makes side-by-side GIFs, `gif_audit.py` one sheet of many.
- Clash GIFs (6.62): Gaha against the Bull Commander shows Suleiman's GIFs as framed panels at the clash opening (src/clashGif.js; frames cut by `~/.fabien-tools/venv/bin/python -I dev/tools/make_clash_gifs.py [<folder>]` into public/art/clashgif and src/data/clashGifs.json). `node dev/browser/clashgif.cjs <out dir> [phone|desktop|both]` starts that clash on the real board, photographs the panels and measures them. These are painted scenes on a brown floor (not black), so make_gif_fx.py is not for them.
- PDFs: they can be read on this Mac. `~/.fabien-tools/venv/bin/python -I dev/tools/pdf_text.py <file.pdf>` prints the text of ReportLab PDFs (all Suleiman's design sheets so far); no poppler needed.
- Waterfalls (1.48): the game shows Suleiman's own (`dev/tools/make_water_s.py` cuts his GIF); `?water=blender` in the address shows the Blender loops. `node dev/browser/water_pair.cjs <out dir>` films both side by side.
- Screen sizes (M7, checklist D4 and D5): `node dev/browser/m7.cjs <out dir> [phone|desktop|both]` measures every interface word (11 real pixels or more), menu rows (44 or more) and that the menu's corner never moves; it also photographs the card, the skills list and the settings sheet.
- Balance: `bash dev/tests/bot_many.sh [runs] [battles each]` plays bot battles side by side and prints who won (the smarter test player is dev/tests/bot_brain.cjs; `node bot.cjs 2 mixed` is the old plain one).
- Desktop (M8, checklist E): `node dev/browser/desktop.cjs <out dir>` measures figure height, the title, story and result on 1440 x 900, and presses hover, Esc, Enter and Space. `node dev/browser/ending.cjs`, `weight.cjs` and `team_colours.cjs` photograph the ending, a hit and the enemy colours.
- Character select (18.30, 18.45): `node dev/browser/select.cjs <out dir> [phone|desktop|both]` (picks, memory, Reset) and `node dev/browser/swap_sides.cjs <out dir> [phone|desktop|both]` (Rabdul onto the heroes, Gaha onto the enemies, a turn with each, Test mode switch). Any character can fight on either side; the default teams are the 4 v 4 without the kings.
- Pace (18.11): `node dev/browser/pace.cjs [phone|desktop]` times enemy rounds at Normal and Fast (checklist G5). `node dev/browser/lineup.cjs <out dir>` photographs the start of a battle.
- Kings and Layra's blessing (6.83 to 6.89): `~/.fabien-tools/venv/bin/python dev/tools/make_kings.py <work> [alagara|gadara]` cuts the kings (sizes in HAND / HAND_ALAGARA, by eye; a missing source keeps its pictures, KEEP_IF_MISSING; `ONLY=<sheets>` cuts just those). `dev/tools/make_layra_bless.py <work>` cuts her floating sheets. `dev/tools/make_expressions.py` cuts the 21 Nov expression faces (kings wired; Leol's and the Thief's kept for later). Gadara's 31 Oct photos are read from INBOX/2026-11-19/x when INBOX/2026-11-06 is missing (same files). `node dev/browser/king_victory.cjs <out> [phone|desktop]` checks the champion's victory pose on the result screen.
- Pose sizes (18.52): `~/.fabien-tools/venv/bin/python dev/tools/pose_size.py audit|table|pin [ids]` checks every family's body is within 10 percent of standing (head reading, or a size by eye in dev/tools/pose_sizes.json); n99 runs it. After recutting a family it fails until you look (contact sheet next to standing) and run `pin`. The kings are sized at the cut: make_kings.py HAND_ALAGARA.
- Size ruler (roadmap 18.2): `~/.fabien-tools/venv/bin/python dev/tools/ruler.py measure|audit|sheets <folder>`. It writes "ruler" (scale and ground line per picture) in art.json; hand values go in dev/tools/ruler_marks.json, head boxes in ruler_heads.json. Run `measure` after adding or recutting pictures. It also writes "footX" (where the feet are across each picture, checklist B3); `audit` checks heads and feet. `python dev/tools/feet_sheet.py out.jpg src/data/art.json <id>` draws every picture on a tile to check by eye.
- GIF effects are the standard format (15 Nov, roadmap 6.58): Suleiman sends each spell effect as a GIF (8 or 9 frames, one canvas, pure black ground, his own frame delay), one folder per spell. `~/.fabien-tools/venv/bin/python dev/tools/make_gif_fx.py [<folder>]` reads the frames with Pillow, turns the black into see-through (colour to alpha; rembg only if a GIF is not pure black), shrinks them to half size, saves `public/art/fx/<family>_<n>.png`, and writes his frame delay and the anchor into `src/data/fxSlots.json` ("buffs": cast, loop, move, rooted per Verse; "spells": orb, bolt, strike, lance for Layra's Lightning and Wildfire) and `dev/tools/fx_anchors.json`; then `align_fx.py fix <families>` (kind "keep" leaves a beam or streak as drawn). A new GIF = one line in FILES at the top of make_gif_fx.py. The game plays them in src/spellArt.js (buffLoopFx, buffMoveFx, sfxBeam...) with ADD blending and keeps his timing (frameMs). Before and after GIFs: `node dev/browser/gifs.cjs <out> phone <names>` on the old build (git stash, vite build) and the new, then `dev/browser/gif_pair.py <before> <after> <out>`; the 15 Nov pairs are in Fabien Blueprint/SPELL EFFECTS 2026-11-15. A folder whose name does not clearly match a spell in the game is listed for Baiyee, not guessed (Umbra, 6.59).
- Kofi's effects (6.63): `ONLY=<families> ~/.fabien-tools/venv/bin/python dev/tools/make_gif_fx.py "<his folder>"` makes just those families (his four are in FILES), then `align_fx.py fix` and `fx_fit.py`. Renewal is a buff slot (halo loop, spiral cast), Haunt a buff slot too (its loop stands on the haunted unit), the comet a spell slot (spirit_bolt).
- Effect sizes (6.64): after make_gif_fx.py run `~/.fabien-tools/venv/bin/python -I dev/tools/fx_fit.py`; it measures the drawn effect in each part and writes "fit" (size, k body heights, anchor, layer) into fxSlots.json. The game sizes every GIF effect by the fighter's body height (unit.topY - 12), never by the picture.
- Effect flipbooks (roadmap 18.3): `~/.fabien-tools/venv/bin/python dev/tools/align_fx.py audit|sheet <out.jpg>|fix [family]`. A new flipbook gets a line in dev/tools/fx_anchors.json (its anchor and the origin the code uses), then `fix <family>`; n55 fails if an anchor moves more than 3 percent.
- Loading (18.47, 18.48): the title loads nothing, the select loads portraits, Start battle loads the picked fighters (src/battleLoad.js). From the title on, src/prefetch.js quietly downloads the select's portraits, the shared board, effects and HUD pictures, then the fighters of the remembered line-up (the select moves newly picked fighters to the front); the bytes are kept as blobs that artUrl hands to the loader, and Start battle pauses it. `node dev/browser/loadtime.cjs [4g|slow|none]` times a cold phone load (files and MB per phase, the battle's bytes by folder; `DUMP=1` lists the biggest files; `DWELL=<seconds>` waits that long in the story and select before pressing Start battle, as a player would). Every PNG in public/art has a WebP twin the game fetches (src/artUrl.js), lossy at quality 90 (95 or lossless when the picture shows a difference; the pictures are pixel art): after ANY cutter writes or re-cuts a PNG run `~/.fabien-tools/venv/bin/python -I dev/tools/make_webp.py` (only changed pictures; `--check` looks, `--verify` measures; about 6 minutes for all) and commit the .webp files; `dev/tools/webp_sheet.py <out.jpg>` makes the old-next-to-new sheet; n92 fails on a stale twin, n96 tests the downloader. The PNGs stay the masters (tools and jsdom tests read them; node-canvas cannot read WebP) and the build leaves them out of the published game. The Blender waterfall loops load only with ?water=blender.
- Deploy: commit and push to main; check `gh run list --limit 1`.

## Where things live
- src/scenes/BattleScene.js: the battle (turns, moves, skills, enemy AI in enemyAttack, animations).
- src/data: jobs.json (classes, stats, skills), spells.json (every skill's numbers and text), units.json (who fights), art.json (every picture per character: standing, battle, poses, moves, "enchanted" looks), maps/cavern.json (the game's map) and maps/yoba.json (old map for tests), terrain.json, story.json, dialogue.json.
- src/fxLayers.js: the layers round every effect (flash, floor light, light on fighters, afterglow, energy waves), the colour set per element and the hit levels. src/data/fxSlots.json: slots for Suleiman's buff effect frames.
- src/codeWalk.js: the walk made in code for fighters without walk pictures, and Layra's sand walk (M13). `node dev/browser/walk_sheet.cjs <out dir>` makes a walk sheet per fighter.
- src/domHud.js (18.44): the battle HUD in HTML and CSS over the canvas (title, turn strip, diamond list, turn panel, card, menu and lists, skill panel, hint, settings, tooltips, clash meter). BattleScene still builds the HUD objects and places them (tests read them); objects marked dom() in buildHud are not drawn on the canvas, and the overlay copies them every frame and taps the same handlers. Not made in jsdom (the tests see the canvas HUD as before). `node dev/browser/hud.cjs <out dir> [phone|desktop|both]` checks it; `node dev/browser/ui_compare.cjs <out dir>` makes the side-by-side sheets with his mock-ups (phone, desktop, Retina).
- src/status.js (buffs, statuses), src/combat.js (damage, line of sight), src/engagement.js (clashes), src/art.js (picks pictures), src/spellArt.js (effects), src/boardArt.js (map drawing, lights), src/enemyAi.js (where enemies walk).
- dev/tools: Python cutters that turn Suleiman's sheets into game pictures (make_*.py; fxcut.cut, iso31.clean and cells are the helpers). public/art: the pictures.
- HOW-TO-EDIT-THE-STORY.md: plain guide to the data files, one section per batch.

## Gotchas
- A figure's sprite size must match its picture: the bot player prints SIZE OFF when `sprite.scaleX` is not `unit.artScale` (1.54). A picture with no "scale" in the ruler is drawn at 1.0, so a pose that is a copy of another look (Tayo's Fire battle stance) needs its own scale pinned in dev/tools/ruler_marks.json.
- Older tests read every number on the unit card at once: boot.cjs sets `b.cardOpen = 'all'`. In the game the card turns pages (`cardOpen` false or true, M7).
- A long callout (`floatText` with a space in it or over 7 letters) goes to the lane under the turn strip, not over the fighter (M6); it makes two Text objects in one call, so a test that fakes `Math.random` must leave text names alone (see n28).
- Tests under load switch Effects to Light by themselves (the frame rate watcher): run n46 and timing tests (n61, n63) alone, not beside bot battles.
- Background removal for new cuts: try rembg first (dev/tools/bgremove.py, installed in ~/.fabien-tools/venv; roadmap 1.36). It is clearly better on smoke and effect pictures on a plain background. Keep the colour-threshold cutters where a floor tile is in the picture (rembg keeps the tile): the sand impact cut. Check any cut on a contact sheet, old next to new.
- Never move a figure's picture up or down with a relative tween (`y: '-=3'`) where two can overlap: they stack and leave it off the ground. codeWalk.js sets the lift from the resting place (`unit.restY`); wading moves that resting place too.
- Layra's Lightning now waits `CHANNEL_MS` (280 ms) before the bolt: a test that times her attack must allow for it (`b.channelLead(unit, target)`).
- Tests must not measure a figure's drawn height directly: figures breathe (up to 3.5 percent taller at a random moment). Measure through the sideways scale (see n37). In background shell loops, never wait with `pgrep -f`: it matches its own command line and never ends.
- Never put `//` comments inside a one-line sed edit to test files: it swallows the rest of the line. Use `/* */`.
- When adding pictures to a character, tests that count its pictures (poison.cjs, echo.cjs, gaha.cjs) need the new count.
- A unit's pictures are not all drawn at one scale any more: `unit.baseScale` is its one size, `unit.artScale` is that times the ruler's scale for the picture shown now. Tests compare with `artScale`, read while breathing rests.
- Phaser Graphics scale from their own x,y: draw shapes at (0,0) and place the Graphics, or they slide when tweened.
- Enemies face the heroes with their upLeft/downLeft pictures; the other facings are mirrored.
- Tests must never make `Math.random` return a constant (or only tiny numbers): Phaser names text pictures with random numbers, and the next text crashes with "reading 'context'".
- A four-view sheet's front figures do not always run down-left then down-right (the archer's bow sheets both point right, 1.45). Look at a contact sheet before wiring; `"flip": true` in art.json mirrors one view.
- Facing check (1.64): `ruler.py measure` ends with `dev/tools/facing_check.py`, which fails when a left view and its right view face one way (a left view should be its right view flipped). Mirrored sheets from Suleiman: cut only the true views and let the game mirror. Look with `facing_check.py sheet <out.jpg> <id or file prefix>` (NW, NE, SW, SE under each cell). Pairs known on 19 Nov are in dev/tools/facing_known.json ("fix" or "ok"); never add a new pair there to make it pass without looking.
- The cavern is 11 by 11 since roadmap 14.2 (it was 9 by 9): cavern tests stand fighters one tile further in than the old map (the old middle sits at +1,+1), pots are at (1,2), (9,2), (1,8), (9,8). `node dev/browser/board.cjs <out dir> [prefix]` photographs the board on phone and desktop and checks it fits. The enemy AI measures walking distance (enemyAi.js walkMapTo), not straight lines.
- The cavern is 4 v 4 now (Kofi h4 and the bull e2; M10b). Cavern tests that set `FABIEN_BENCH=` get all 8; `FABIEN_BENCH=e2,h4` is the old 3 v 3. The old test map (yoba) is still 3 v 3 (Kofi is benched in units.json).
- Kofi's head cannot be measured on his casting sheets (glowing orbs): new Kofi sheets are cut at one hand-set scale and pinned at 1.0 in ruler_marks.json. A word (not a picture) added to a fighter in art.json must go in SKIP in dev/tools/ruler_lib.py.
- Between turns an archer swaps dagger and bow by herself (`readyCloseWeapons`, 1.46), and her AI stabs a foe next to her before walking. A test that needs her to keep the bow keeps foes 2 tiles away.
- `landHit` shows a hidden unit again (1.47). It does not take the health off: the caller does.
- An enemy that has acted may walk again with its leftover steps (13.53). A test that needs it to stay put sets `b.enemySecondWalk = false`.
- The HTML HUD (18.44): a press on an HTML panel must stop there (domHud stops touchstart and mousedown), or Phaser reads it as a tap outside the canvas and cancels the choice. A new HUD part: build it in BattleScene as before, mark it with dom(), give the overlay a sync function that reads it. Board hover checks `domHud.overPanel`. A new font weight must be added to the list domHud loads at the start.
- With `b.setSpeed` above 1, `knockUntil` still waits in real milliseconds (the scene clock is not scaled): tests wait on `unit.knock`, and M11's fast speed must scale it.
- Never use `git stash` or `git stash pop` in this repo (21 Nov: a `stash pop` with nothing to stash popped an old stash and left conflict markers in six files). To compare with the old build, use `git worktree add` or a fresh clone in the scratchpad.
